## Research

### DeepSeek Harness Plugin Ecosystem Security Scan

**September 2, 2026**

AgentGuard sampled 659 DeepSeek Harness plugins representing 90.0% of marketplace downloads over the previous 30 days.

Among 590 successfully scanned plugins, 94.7% were rated High or Critical based on capability surface, permission exposure, and potential impact.
